Project synopsis

Milky Way is a browser-based audio instrument where the solar system is the interface. Each planet is its own note, and the Sun is the start and stop button for a looping tune. Tapping a planet plays it and sends coloured ripples across the sky. Holding a planet and dragging it up or down changes its pitch, shown with glowing pitch lines and a trail of dots. Big pitch buttons move every note higher or lower, and a field of stars in the background glows in response to every touch and every ripple.

It is built with HTML, CSS, SVG and JavaScript, using Tone.js for the sound.

Design scenario

My design scenario is an audio instrument for a toddler: someone who can't read yet, has small hands, taps and drags with whole fingers, and learns by trying things and seeing what happens. The instrument also had to work on whatever screen it was opened on, whether that was a parent's phone, a tablet or a laptop.

How the finished project responds to the scenario

  • No reading needed. The instructions are a large, glowing banner with a big icon, and a pulsing ring and bouncing hand show exactly what to touch next. The steps change as the child plays.
  • Nothing can sound wrong. Every note comes from a pentatonic scale, so any combination of planets sounds pleasant.
  • Every touch gets a reaction. Sound, ripples, glowing planets and twinkling stars all respond straight away, which teaches cause and effect.
  • Big, forgiving targets. Planets and buttons are large, and a quick tap is held long enough to be heard.
  • Adaptive layout. The sky scales to any screen size, the layout is a column that rearranges on short screens, touch dragging doesn't scroll the page, and animations are reduced for people who prefer less motion.

Documentation

The full write-up is on its own page: Final Project Documentation. In short, the core interactive features are:

  • Tap a planet to play it and switch it on or off for the loop.
  • Hold and drag a planet up or down to change its note.
  • Tap the Sun to start and stop the loop.
  • Pitch buttons shift every note up or down an octave.
  • Step-by-step hints guide a toddler through the instrument.
  • Glowing stars react to touch and ripples.

The documentation also covers my extended technique, and the design system (colour, type, spacing, components and responsive behaviour).
